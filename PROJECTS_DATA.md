## Instructions

1. Navigate to `/admin/login` and log in
2. Go to `/admin/projects`
3. Click "Add Project" button
4. Fill in the form with the data above
5. Set "Publish immediately" checkbox if you want the project to be visible
6. Click "Create Project"
7. Repeat for all projects

**Note:** You can add image URLs later when you have the poster images uploaded to a hosting service or in your public folder.
